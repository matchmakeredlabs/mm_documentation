## What is MatchMaker?
MatchMaker is revolutionary new technology that enables meaningful, valid and reliable matches between learning assets, competency frameworks, and curricula. MatchMake can used for higher education, human resources, personal skill growth, and much more. With MatchMaker, gone are the days of wasting time relearning or struggling to compare different sources of knowledge!

## How does MatchMaker work? 
### The Power of Collections
MatchMaker is powered by a novel and intuitive architecture for storing learning assets, competency-based frameworks, and curricula. This architecture generalizes all of these items into what are known as __collections__. Let's take this documentation and turn it into a collection! 
<div style={{textAlign: "center"}}>
<img src={ElementsIntro?.src || ElementsIntro} width="43%" alt="Inside each collection sits elements which are made up of two colors which mix to create the collection's color"/>
</div>
In the figure above, this section of documentation is organized into a collection. Collections consist of elements, which are organized in a hierarchy. At the moment, there are only two elements: (1) "Introduction", organized under the (2) "Getting Started" element. Notice that "Introduction" has a particular color within the collection:<div style={{color: "green", margin: 0, display: 'contents'}}>green</div>. In color theory, it is always possible to create a color out of a sum of others. In MatchMaker, this principle is applied to knowledge. Therefore, upon looking closer, this green is actually made up of a mix of blue and yellow. In essence, the overall color, green, represents exactly what this introduction section teaches. Noting, the green has been split up into two pieces which combine to fully represent the whole. This is where the real magic of MatchMaker lies, as each element at the bottom of a hierarchy must be made of different components drawn from what is called the Palet<sup style={{fontSize: 8}}>TM</sup>.

### What is the Palet?
The Palet<sup style={{fontSize: 8}}>TM</sup> is a MMEdLabs' maintained database of little bits of knowledge you can use to describe any piece of learning material. The Palet, serving as the backbone for all matches between items, fuels many of MM’s innovative features. Each tiny piece of knowledge in the Palet is referred to as a Palet statement, and an element's unique combination of Palet statements is called a Palet Key<sup style={{fontSize: 8}}>TM</sup>. Think of the Palet as the many different colors you can choose from to paint your elements, a Palet statement as a particular color, and a Palet Key as a particular art piece you make from a choice of colors.
