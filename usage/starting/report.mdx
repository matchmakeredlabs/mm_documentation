Now that you've learned how to create a collection, it's time to get down to the heart of MatchMaker, gaining valuable insights by matching between collections. You'll learn how to generate a report in which every element has insightful matches in to other collections you've selected. This is where the real power of MatchMaker begins (also where your work ends).

There's a lot of potential for customization in this section. For now, let's get you up and running. It's important to know two pieces of vocabulary before we begin. When you match collections in MatchMaker, there's two types of collections: 
1. __Anchored__ - anchoring occurs when individuals use a particular collection(s)—the "anchor"— as a reference point to make subsequent matches. In this example, Algebra 1, the mandatory curriculum that reflects the content to be mastered by students, serves as the anchored collection. 

2. __Responding__ - the responding collection(s) reacts to the anchored collection(s) by providing meaningful matches. In this example, Math Help, a resource that supports Malia's Algebra 1 curriculum, is the responding collection.

When two elements match one another the ranking of the match is indicated by the MatchIndex. As with all indices there are no units associated with this number. It represents the relative strength of the match compared to other matches to the same element.  Matches in the MatchMaker interface are organized with the highest MatchIndex at the top of the list decreasing from there. In this example, “Completing the Square” sits at the top with the highest MatchIndex; this is intuitive, as that element is a direct explanation of a method of Solving Quadratic Equations. Furthermore, the other two elements in Math Help sit a little lower as they don't directly address the topic as well. Using the MatchIndex to rank responses increases the probability that Shmingus will see which Math Help element is most likely to be helpful. MatchMaker functionality can be easily into the Math Help site to make John Shmingus aware of this.
