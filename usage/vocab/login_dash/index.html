Login

This is the first page you’ll see when you open MatchMaker.

Type in your provided username into the Username field at the top, and your provided password in the Password field right below. Finally, press Login to start using MatchMaker. Note, your session will expire in 24 hours after your login.

Dashboard

This is your portal to MatchMaker: the dashboard. Note you may see fewer buttons than the ones shown below, depending on your permissions. If you think you should have access to a button but do not see it, please contact your administrator.


Create
Create, register, and manage collections

Browse & Describe
Browse and Describe Available Elements

Generate Report
Generate reports from described elements

Administer
Manage collaboration settings

Click on any of the above buttons to learn more about that section. Here’s a quick summary:

Create​

Create and edit collections you have access to, either creating or modifying a hierarchy.

Browse & Describe​

View all available collections, and start describing elements in collections you have such access to.

Generate Report​

Gain insights from matching collections and custom sets to one another. Additionally, you can create and edit custom edits in this section.

Administer​

The center for all permission management. Here you can manage users, organizations you have access to, and groups.
